He ultimately decided he still loves the game, still wants to compete, and believes he can help the 76ers win a title. *(CBS Sports, NBC.com)*
  - *Why it matters:* This reframes the whole 25-day wait — LeBron wasn't just choosing a team; he was deciding whether to play at all. The Sixers didn't just win the sweepstakes, they convinced him to play one more run.

- **BRONNY NOT A PACKAGE DEAL — LeBRON ADDRESSES JAYLEN BROWN SITUATION.** Rich Paul confirmed to ESPN that Bronny James will not be following his father to Philadelphia — the two are "not a package deal" and there are no current plans for Bronny to join. Separately, LeBron directly addressed the resurfaced Jaylen Brown clip where Brown said "I don't think Bronny is a pro." *(AOL/various)*
  - *Why it matters:* The Bronny angle was always part of the Lakers narrative. That chapter is closed. And the Brown-Bronny awkwardness is the feel-good sidebar of the day — now teammates, now linked.

- **DOMINOES UNLOCKED: HARDEN, DRAYMOND, KUMINGA ALL FREE TO MOVE.** With LeBron off the board, every team he was connected to can now proceed with their own offseason. James Harden is free to finalize his new deal in Cleveland without cap complications from a potential LeBron signing. Draymond Green's expected re-sign with Golden State can close. The Jonathan Kuminga negotiation opens up too. *(ESPN, CBS Sports)*
  - *Why it matters:* The entire NBA was frozen behind one man for 25 days. That freeze ended Friday evening. Expect a flurry of signings over the weekend.
