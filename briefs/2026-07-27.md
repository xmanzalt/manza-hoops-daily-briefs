## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES

- **NUGGETS MATCH THUNDER'S OFFER SHEET FOR SPENCER JONES — AND BLOW PAST THE SECOND APRON.** Oklahoma City signed Denver's restricted free agent to a fully-guaranteed **2-year, $12 million** offer sheet on Saturday. Denver had until 11:59 p.m. ET Sunday and matched. The cost: Denver's luxury tax bill jumps from roughly **$36 million to $68 million**, and the team crosses into the **second apron**. *(NBA.com, Denver Gazette, CBS Sports)*
  - *Why it matters:* This is the sneakiest-good story of the weekend. OKC weaponized the cap against a rival — they knew Denver couldn't afford to lose a rotation wing for nothing, and they made them pay $32M in tax for a guy who averaged 5.5 points. The Thunder get their taxpayer MLE back. Denver is now locked into second-apron restrictions all season.

- **LEBRON OFFICIALLY SIGNS WITH PHILADELPHIA — WITH A 15% TRADE KICKER.** Shams confirmed Sunday that James has formally put pen to paper: **2 years, $8 million, player option in Year 2, plus a full 15% trade kicker.** The near-minimum money is now official league business, not just a report. *(Shams Charania, HoopsHype, NBC Sports)*
  - *Why it matters:* The trade kicker on a minimum-level deal is unusual and worth flagging — it's a small piece of leverage LeBron kept for himself. He also said on signing: "I still want to sacrifice. I still want to work. I still want to grind."

- **KENTAVIOUS CALDWELL-POPE BOUGHT OUT BY MEMPHIS — HEADING TO THE 76ERS.** KCP agreed to a buyout with the Grizzlies and is signing a **1-year, $3.9 million** deal with Philadelphia, reuniting with LeBron five years after their 2020 Lakers championship. To clear the roster spot, Philly waived Dalen Terry off a non-guaranteed $2.6M. *(NBC Sports Philadelphia, Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* First domino to actually fall behind LeBron — and it's a LeBron-adjacent one. Philly is building a veteran, win-now shell around him fast.

- **KAWHI INVESTIGATION: FACT-FINDING IS DONE, DISCIPLINE PHASE BEGINS.** The outside law firm hired by the NBA has reportedly **completed its work**. The league now reviews the findings and decides whether Kawhi Leonard, the Clippers, or owner Steve Ballmer face discipline. The agreed Raptors trade still cannot proceed. *(Yahoo Sports, CBS Sports, Basketball Forever)*
  - *Why it matters:* Biggest movement in weeks on the biggest unresolved story in the league — but "fact-finding complete" is not "resolved." Possible appeals could still drag this out.
