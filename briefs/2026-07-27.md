  - *Why it matters:* Draymond's return keeps the Warriors' core intact alongside Curry, Porzingis, and Horford. The deal isn't officially signed yet — but every report says it's a matter of when, not if.

- **MIAMI HEAT PIVOT HARD — FAVORITES FOR DEROZAN AND/OR BEAL.** After missing on LeBron, the Heat redirected immediately. Miami is now viewed by rival teams as the frontrunner to sign DeMar DeRozan and/or Bradley Beal, both unrestricted free agents, and is also monitoring Klay Thompson. Reports say Miami is "very likely" to land at least one of the two veteran All-Stars. *(Bleacher Report, HoopsRumors, Yahoo Sports, ClutchPoints)*
  - *Why it matters:* The Heat already have Giannis and Bam Adebayo. Adding two of DeRozan/Beal/Klay would make Miami a legitimate championship threat — possibly the East's best roster by week's end.
