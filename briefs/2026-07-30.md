## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES

- **KAWHI PROBE COULD DRAG INTO 2027 — ESPN.** The NBA's investigation into whether the Clippers circumvented the salary cap to pay Kawhi Leonard through the bankrupt green-banking company Aspiration **may not resolve until 2027** if the parties can't agree on the findings or a settlement. Outside counsel Wachtell Lipton has *expanded* the probe to include a previously unreported second endorsement deal involving Leonard, plus team expenses paid on his behalf that the Clippers were never reimbursed for. *(ESPN, Yahoo Sports)*
  - *Why it matters:* This is the opposite of what was reported over the weekend. If the Clippers or the players' union reject the league's findings, it goes to a jointly appointed arbitrator — and then a three-person appeals panel whose ruling is binding. That's a process measured in months, not days. The Kawhi-to-Toronto trade stays frozen the entire time.

- **NBPA IS DIGGING IN — "NOT FROM ANYTHING I'VE SEEN."** Players' association executive director David Kelly, asked whether he'd seen evidence that warranted punishing the Clippers, said: "Not from anything I've seen." Sources indicate the union would take the matter to arbitration rather than accept penalties without sufficient proof. *(ESPN)*
  - *Why it matters:* The union is the reason this could reach 2027. Adam Silver said earlier this month he was "hopeful" for a resolution before the season. That hope now looks optimistic.

- **DRAYMOND GREEN OFFICIALLY BACK WITH THE WARRIORS — 1 YEAR, $27.7M.** Green agreed to a one-year deal that essentially matches the player option he declined last month to give Golden State cap flexibility for its failed LeBron pursuit. Reported July 28 by ESPN's Shams Charania and confirmed by AP sources. *(ESPN, AP, NBA.com, Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* Season 15 in the Bay. If he plays 51 games, he joins Stephen Curry as the only players in franchise history to reach 1,000 games with the Warriors. The one-year term is the story — it sets up either a retirement decision or a 2027 free agency exit.

- **NUGGETS SLASH THE PEYTON WATSON PRICE.** Denver has dropped its sign-and-trade asking price for restricted free agent Peyton Watson from two first-round picks plus two swaps down to **one "high-level player" plus one first-rounder**, per ESPN. The Bucks, Clippers and Hawks have been linked. Denver is also reportedly trying to offload one or two contracts to create room to re-sign him. *(ESPN via ClutchPoints, Yahoo Sports, Last Word On Basketball)*
  - *Why it matters:* Denver matched the Spencer Jones offer sheet on Sunday and jumped into the second apron doing it. Now they're cutting the Watson price and shopping salary. That's a front office feeling the apron squeeze in real time.

## 🏀 FREE AGENCY UPDATE

The post-LeBron wave has slowed into a grind. Draymond got done. Harden didn't. The top of the market — DeRozan, Beal, Kuminga, Klay — is all still open on Day 30, and the reason is mechanics, not interest: Kuminga needs a sign-and-trade with a cooperative third team, Klay needs Dallas to accept a buyout it doesn't want, and Miami needs to move salary before it can absorb anyone meaningful.

**Market snapshot:** Washington quietly became the most interesting new bidder — Trae Young on a max, Anthony Davis, the No. 1 pick in AJ Dybantsa, and now in on DeRozan. Cleveland is juggling Harden's number, Kuminga's price, and a stuck Hezonja signing while trying to stay under the second apron. Miami has about $8M of mid-level left and a Klay obsession. Denver is selling instead of buying.
