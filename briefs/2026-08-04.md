- **THE JALEN DUREN IMPASSE NOW HAS NUMBERS — AND THEY'RE FAR APART.** ESPN's Ramona Shelburne confirmed the Duren-Detroit standoff has hit a full "standstill," with Duren seeking something closer to **five years / $200M+** while the Pistons want to stay in the **mid-$30M/year** range. - **DeMar DeROZAN "EXPECTED TO SIGN THIS WEEK" — AND THE PISTONS ARE NOW IN THE MIX.** NBC Sports reports DeRozan is expected to finalize a deal "this week," while Larry Brown Sports flags Detroit as a "surprising new team" now linked.   - *Why it matters:* The Pistons angle came out of nowhere — Detroit has reportedly been evaluating DeRozan as a veteran scoring punch for a young team. Washington still has the cleanest money ($11.5M tax space + full ~$15M non-taxpayer MLE). If this lands somewhere unexpected, it's a video right there.

- **STEPHEN CURRY EXTENSION TALK HEATS UP — HE'S ELIGIBLE AUGUST 29.** Multiple sources confirm the Warriors and Curry have **mutual interest** in a max extension (up to 2 years/$136.7M), and the Warriors are open to a 1-year or 2-year deal. Curry is entering the final year of his current deal at $62.6M in 2026-27. No deal can be signed until **August 29**. *(Bleacher Report, Yahoo Sports, Hoops Wire)*
  - *Why it matters:* This is the next big shoe to drop — the greatest shooter in NBA history and the franchise that built itself around him, locked in a summer negotiation. At 38, the structure (one year vs. two) could determine whether Golden State is a contender or a transition team in 2027-28.

- **ESPN'S BOBBY MARKS: "FREE AGENCY IS ESSENTIALLY OVER" — AND 10 TRENDS THAT DEFINED IT.** ESPN's salary cap analyst published a blockbuster fact-vs.-fiction piece August 3, auditing the entire offseason. Key findings: only **three teams** (Nets, Bulls, Lakers) had genuine cap room this summer; Wembanyama left **$50M on the table** by not including a 30% escalator in his max extension; and the current CBA is accelerating extensions over traditional free agency across the league. *(ESPN / Bobby Marks)*
  - *Why it matters:* This is a great on-camera explainer framework — the offseason wasn't just about big deals, it was about a structural shift in how NBA rosters get built. The fact that only three teams had cap room explains why so many veterans are still sitting unsigned at Day 34.

Three big names just signed, most of the unresolved names have been unresolved for weeks, and the next major event on the calendar is Curry's extension eligibility on August 29.
