# 🏀 MANZA HOOPS — DAILY NBA CUE SHEET

*Covers the full US NBA day of Tuesday, August 4, 2026. No signings of consequence — but a genuinely useful day, because Marc Stein spent it killing three rumors your competitors are still reporting as real. Portland is not chasing DeRozan. Jrue Holiday is not getting bought out. And the actual numbers behind the two biggest standoffs in the league — Duren and Watson — finally leaked, and they're both smaller than the internet has been telling you.*

> ⚠️ **HEADS-UP BEFORE RECORDING:**
> 1. **TODAY IS A DEBUNKING DAY — LEAN INTO IT.** Three of the biggest talking points on NBA social this week got shot down by one reporter in one newsletter. That's your video. "Everything you heard this week was wrong" is a stronger hook than any transaction on the board.
> 2. **I GAVE YOU A SOFT NUMBER ON PEYTON WATSON — HERE IS THE REAL ONE.** Yesterday's brief said Denver's offer was **5 years / $70M (~$14M a year)**. Stein's reporting is **four years / roughly $70M**, and he says he has "consistently heard" Denver's offers land in the **$16–18M per year** range. That's meaningfully more money than the number going around. Use **$16–18M AAV**.

The date is **November 2, 2026** — and the news is that **Billups' own lawyers filed to keep it**, because *prosecutors* wanted it pushed to early 2027. He's the one demanding to go to trial sooner. Don't flip that around.

> 5. **DE'ANTHONY MELTON IS NOT NEW.** You may see his name in Tuesday round-ups. He re-signed with Golden State on **July 3** — two years, about **$11.2M** with a player option. Some write-ups list $12.46M. Use **$11.2M**, and don't present it as fresh.
> 6. **THE LUKA STORY IS YOUR EASY EUROPEAN ANGLE.** He says he's **100 percent healthy**, and he's flying **the entire Lakers roster — including two-way players — to Slovenia** on his own dime later this month. A European star hosting an NBA team in Europe is a story you can tell from Lithuania better than anyone in Los Angeles can.

## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES

- **MARC STEIN KILLS THE DEROZAN-TO-PORTLAND STORY — AND THE JRUE HOLIDAY BUYOUT WITH IT.** Stein reported Tuesday that despite heavy online speculation, **Portland is not pursuing DeMar DeRozan**, and there is **no credible indication** the Blazers would buy out **Jrue Holiday** or that Holiday wants one. The Blazers have been "messaging for some time" that keeping Holiday is the preference. He's owed **$34.8M in 2026-27** with a **$37.2M player option** for 2027-28. *(Marc Stein via Hoops Rumors, NBC Sports)*
  - *Why it matters:* Portland only has **12 fully guaranteed contracts** and an obvious need for forward depth, which is exactly why the internet invented this. It's the second DeRozan rumor in a week to collapse — the Chicago report from July 30 is *still* unverified. DeRozan has been "expected to sign this week" for about ten days now. Report the market, not the deal.

- **THE REAL NUMBERS IN THE PEYTON WATSON STANDOFF ARE OUT — AND THEY'RE HIGHER THAN REPORTED.** Stein says Denver's offers to its restricted free agent have "consistently" been in the **$16–18M per year** range, with the best formal offer around **four years / $70M**. Watson has been seeking **north of $25M/yr**. Off the court he's bulked up to **220 pounds**, from a listed 200 last season. *(Marc Stein via Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* This reframes the whole standoff. The story all summer was "Denver is lowballing him at fourteen million." It isn't — it's offering close to the full mid-level annually. The gap is still real, roughly **$8M a year**, but this is two sides who disagree about a player's tier, not a team refusing to pay. And the structural blocker hasn't moved: **Denver is over the second apron and cannot take salary back** in a sign-and-trade until it clears room.

- **DETROIT'S ACTUAL OFFER TO JALEN DUREN LEAKS: $35–38M PER YEAR.** Reports Tuesday put the Pistons' offer to their restricted free agent in the **$35–38M annually** range. Duren — an **All-Star and All-NBA selection** last season — is max-eligible at **five years and up to $287.1M**. He's now more than a month into restricted free agency unsigned, and Stein says he "has been weighing the unappealing prospect of playing out the 2026-27 season on a qualifying offer" of just under **$10M**. *(Larry Brown Sports, Heavy, Marc Stein)*
  - *Why it matters:* Detroit is not being cheap — $35–38M a year is a top-of-market center salary. But Duren made **All-NBA**, and All-NBA players get the max. That's the entire argument in one sentence. And the qualifying-offer threat is the real leverage: take ~$10M for one year, gain a **no-trade veto**, and hit unrestricted free agency in 2027 with Detroit's matching rights gone.

- **LUKA DONCIC SAYS HE'S "100 PERCENT" HEALTHY — AND IS FLYING THE ENTIRE LAKERS ROSTER TO SLOVENIA.** In an email to fans Tuesday, Doncic said: *"I took a real break from basketball the first few weeks… My recovery went great, and I'm 100 percent healthy again."* He missed the 2026 playoffs with a **Grade 2 hamstring strain** suffered in April. Separately, his **four-day Slovenia mini-camp** happens later this month — workouts, golf, and Ljubljana team bonding — and he is **paying for all of it**, extending the invite to **two-way players** as well. Every current teammate is going except first-rounder **Cameron Carr**, who has NBA rookie orientation. *(Hoops Rumors, Marc Stein, NBC Sports)*
  - *Why it matters:* He averaged **33.5 points, 7.7 rebounds and 8.3 assists**, made **First Team All-NBA** and finished **fourth in MVP voting** — then watched the postseason in a suit. This is the first Lakers season that is unambiguously his, with LeBron in Philadelphia and eight new players in the building, and his first act as the franchise is a European team-bonding trip he's funding himself. That's a leadership statement, not a vacation.

## 🏀 FREE AGENCY UPDATE

Day 36, and Tuesday produced exactly **two NBA contracts** — both by the Toronto Raptors, both worth close to nothing. That's the state of the market. What Tuesday actually delivered was **information**: the real numbers behind the standoffs, and three rumors killed.

The six names still on the board are unchanged, and every one is stuck on a mechanic rather than a market:

- **Harden** — agreed with Cleveland; **structure** is the holdup, because how the money spreads sets Cleveland's second-apron room. Cleveland is also chasing Watson and Kuminga, which is part of why the Harden paperwork keeps waiting.
- **Duren** — Detroit at **$35–38M/yr**, Duren at the max. The **~$10M qualifying offer** is live and it is real leverage.
- **Watson** — Denver at **$16–18M/yr**; Watson wants **$25M+**. Denver is **over the second apron and cannot take salary back**. Bucks, Cavs, Hawks and Clippers circling.
- **Kuminga** — the Lakers have reportedly offered **two years, $20M total**. He wants **north of the $15M mid-level** annually. Atlanta holds the veto — and the reported blocker in the Lakers' version is that **Atlanta doesn't want two years of Jarred Vanderbilt's money**.
- **Mathurin** — **Chicago, New Orleans, Milwaukee and Dallas** have all approached the Clippers about a sign-and-trade. Still no offer sheet filed.
- **DeRozan** — suitor list keeps *shrinking*: Portland is out, Chicago never confirmed.

**Market snapshot:** Sacramento is shopping for a **veteran point guard to mentor rookie Darius Acuff Jr.** — and **Russell Westbrook** (who was on the Kings last season) and **Victor Oladipo** are on that list. The Lakers still want a starting power forward and are working three names: **Kuminga**, **P.J. Washington**, and **De'Andre Hunter** as the fallback. Philadelphia is operating **$1.8M below the first apron** hard cap. New York has **13 guaranteed players** and is in no hurry.
