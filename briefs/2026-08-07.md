## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES

- **THE NBA ANNOUNCES 2026-27 OPENING NIGHT: KNICKS VS. 76ERS.** The league confirmed Thursday: the new season tips off **October 20** with New York hosting Philadelphia — a direct rematch of last spring's sweep that gave the Knicks their first title since 1973. The Sixers return with **LeBron James and Jaylen Brown**. Christmas Day is confirmed: **Cavs at Knicks (12pm), Spurs at Thunder (2:30pm), Mavs at Warriors (5pm), Rockets at Lakers (8pm), Wolves at Nuggets (10:30pm)** — all ABC/ESPN. Full schedule drops **August 13**. *(Fox News, AOL Sports, CBS Sports)*
  - *Why it matters:* Year one of the new NBC/Peacock/Amazon/ESPN media deal, and the league opens with its biggest narrative: a rematch of the most dramatic playoff series of 2026 with LeBron now on the other side.

- **DILLON BROOKS LOCKS UP WITH THE SUNS: 3 YEARS, $73 MILLION.** Phoenix secures its enforcer on a team-friendly extension through 2029-30. Brooks posted a career-high **20.2 PPG** in his first Phoenix season. The internet's reaction — one headline ran "**$73 Million to Be Annoying**" — went massively viral. *(Washington Post, Yahoo Sports, NBA.com, Pro Football Network)*
  - *Why it matters:* Biggest confirmed signing in over a week. Phoenix is committing to its current core. The debate over whether an enforcer who scores 20 points deserves $73M is genuinely split — and it's the most-discussed contract of Thursday.

- **MAVERICKS DELIBERATELY BLOCKING A KLAY THOMPSON BUYOUT — BECAUSE OF THE LAKERS.** New reporting confirms Dallas is *intentionally* resisting a buyout precisely because Lakers interest is real and they believe they can get trade assets in return. Thompson wants to play in Los Angeles (owns a home there). Heat interest is confirmed. Without a buyout, Klay sits on Dallas' books at **$17.5M** while they wait for a trade offer. Dallas currently has 16 players on 15 roster spots — something must move before opening night either way. *(Yardbarker, HITC, Heavy, ClutchPoints)*
  - *Why it matters:* Reframes the whole story from "when will he be bought out?" to "what will the Lakers give up?" If Los Angeles wants Thompson badly enough, Dallas extracts a draft pick. If not, the Mavs eventually have to blink.

- **ANTHONY DAVIS NOW EXTENSION-ELIGIBLE IN WASHINGTON — AND BOTH SIDES AGREED TO WAIT.** August 6 was the first day Davis could negotiate a new deal with the Wizards after the three-year anniversary of his previous Lakers extension. He's eligible for up to **4 years / $275M**. Per Rich Paul and the Wizards front office, serious talks will wait until the season is underway. *(CBS Sports, HoopsHype, Last Word on Basketball)*
  - *Why it matters:* Davis has never played a game for Washington. They gave up major assets to get him at the trade deadline. Tabling this until midseason is rational — but it makes the biggest unsettled contract in the NBA a live in-season storyline all year.
