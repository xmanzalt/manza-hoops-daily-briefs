## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES

- **DON NELSON, HALL OF FAMER AND SECOND-WINNINGEST COACH IN NBA HISTORY, DIES AT 86.** Nelson passed away **Sunday morning** surrounded by family, per a family statement. He won **five championships as a player with the Celtics**, was a **three-time Coach of the Year**, was inducted into the Hall of Fame in **2012**, and finished with **1,335 career wins** — second all-time behind Gregg Popovich. He never won a title as a head coach. *(NBA.com, ESPN, CBS Sports, CNN, Yahoo Sports)*
  - *Why it matters:* This is the lead, and it's not close. Nelson didn't just win — he **invented the modern NBA**. The "point forward" (Paul Pressey, Marques Johnson in Milwaukee), small-ball, positionless lineups, Hack-a-Shaq: all Nellie. Every team playing five-out today is running his offense. He coached the Bucks, Warriors (11 years across two stints), Knicks and Mavericks — and he's the guy who bet on a skinny 20-year-old German nobody wanted.

- **BEN SIMMONS IS ACTUALLY COMING BACK — FULL-GO, ZERO RESTRICTIONS.** Simmons will be a **full participant with "zero restrictions"** in Australia's player-led national team mini-camp in **Melbourne, starting Monday** — and he **paid his own way in**. He sat out the entire 2025-26 season to reset his body and has reportedly regained his athleticism. Per Marc J. Spears (Andscape), **several NBA teams have expressed interest**, including a **Western Conference team that has already offered a training camp invite**. Simmons is open to a **veteran's minimum** deal and is targeting the **2028 Olympics** with the Boomers. *(Andscape via Hoops Rumors, HoopsHype, Yahoo Sports)*
  - *Why it matters:* Simmons hasn't been able to prove *health* in years — that's the whole thing. If he gets through a full-contact camp at full capacity, he goes from cautionary tale to a legitimate minimum-salary swing for a contender. At a minimum price, a healthy Simmons is arguably the best value bet left on the board.

- **DAY 41 AND THE RESTRICTED FREE AGENT MARKET IS STILL COMPLETELY FROZEN.** Duren, Watson, Mathurin and Kuminga are **all still unsigned**, and Sunday's reporting made clear why: Denver is in the second apron with a projected **$68M tax bill** after matching OKC's offer sheet for Spencer Jones, the Clippers are stuck until the Kawhi investigation and the Ingram/Dick trade resolve, and Kuminga's market has collapsed to minimum-money offers from Sacramento. *(Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* Four legitimate rotation players — one of them an All-NBA-caliber center — are in limbo in **August**. This is what the second apron actually does to a league, and we're watching it in real time.

- **JOSH HART BECOMES EXTENSION-ELIGIBLE TODAY.** As of **August 10**, the Knicks can offer Hart up to **4 years / $131M**. New York is already navigating second-apron problems and is widely expected to need a team-friendly number from both Hart and Karl-Anthony Towns. *(Hoops Rumors, RotoBaller)*
  - *Why it matters:* The defending champs' entire cap flexibility runs through whether their glue guys take discounts. This is the first real test.
