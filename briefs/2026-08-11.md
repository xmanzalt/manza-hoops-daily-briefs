## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES

- **KAWHI LEONARD SAYS HE EXPECTS HIS TRADE TO THE RAPTORS TO CLOSE BEFORE CAMP.** Per Jake Fischer, Leonard believes the deal agreed in late June — sending him to Toronto for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, two unprotected firsts, a swap and two seconds — will finalize before Raptors camp opens next month. He's still officially a Clipper while the NBA investigates alleged "no-show" endorsement deals with defunct green bank **Aspiration** and scoreboard maker **Daktronics**. Commissioner **Adam Silver** has indicated a resolution could come within **six weeks**, possibly including sanctions against the Clippers. *(Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* Leonard is on a $50.3M expiring deal and becomes an unrestricted free agent next summer without a new contract — Toronto wants this done specifically so it can extend him. Six weeks would put resolution right around training camp.

- **KNICKS' OFFSEASON PICTURE COMES INTO FOCUS: TOWNS, HART, McBRIDE, MATKOVIC, NICKEL.** New York's attempt to trade for Pelicans big man **Karlo Matkovic** fell through. On extensions: Josh Hart became eligible for a **4-year, up to $131M** deal on August 10 but has **not signed yet** — he's expected to take a team-friendly number, but is likely waiting to see how Karl-Anthony Towns' extension talks shake out first. Towns (eligible for up to 4yr/$272M) is expected to get an offer, but **not a max** — likely starting around **$45M** (he holds a $61M player option for 2027-28, so there's no urgency). Miles McBride, on a team-friendly deal, is considered less likely to extend since he's expected to want more money. Second-round pick **Tyler Nickel** is now "leaning toward" signing with New York over a fifth year at Vanderbilt. *(Hoops Rumors, Rotoballer, Spotrac)*
  - *Why it matters:* This is the same second-apron squeeze theme running through the whole offseason — the champs can only make a few of these deals work, and Towns is the one Hart is watching before he commits to a number.

- **PAYTON PRITCHARD SPEAKS ON LOSING JAYLEN BROWN — AND ISN'T RUSHING HIS OWN EXTENSION.** On the July trade that sent Brown to Philadelphia for Paul George, Pritchard said: *"I mean, it's the NBA at the end of the day. It's part of the business. Things change."* He credited Brown as a mentor who *"took me under his wing, pushed me, made me better"* and said he'll be *"missed mightily."* On his own extension (eligible for up to 3yr/$67M): *"I don't really think about it... I have two years on my contract. There's no rush on it."* *(Boston.com, Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* Pritchard, the reigning Sixth Man of the Year who posted career highs (17.0/5.2/4.0) last season, is Boston's next big cap decision — and he's making clear he's in no hurry to take a discount.

- **VICTOR WEMBANYAMA: "I AGREE, YEAH" — HE THINKS HE CAN SCORE 100 IN A GAME.** Asked directly in a Q&A with a French content creator, Wembanyama didn't hesitate. His career high is 50, against Washington in 2024. He also named **Evan Fournier** — fresh off an Olympiacos EuroLeague MVP season — as the French player he'd most want as a teammate. *(Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* Pure content gold, and a reminder Wemby is fully past last season's DVT scare and back to talking like the most audacious offensive weapon in the league.
