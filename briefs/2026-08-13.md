## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES

- **LAKERS SOLD FOR RECORD $12.5 BILLION — BOB IGER AND JOSH KUSHNER TAKE OVER FROM MARK WALTER.** In a stunner, the Lakers are being sold again — barely a year after Mark Walter bought majority control from the Buss family for roughly $10 billion. Former Disney CEO Bob Iger and Thrive Capital's Josh Kushner (Jared Kushner's younger brother) are buying the franchise for a North American sports record $12.5 billion. Reports say Iger and Kushner intend to honor Walter's pact keeping Jeanie Buss on as team governor. The deal still needs NBA Board of Governors approval, expected next month. *(CNBC, ESPN, CBS Sports)*
  - *Why it matters:* This more than doubles the previous Lakers valuation from just last year and instantly becomes the biggest sports-franchise sale in North American history — a massive story regardless of what happens on the court.

- **RUSSELL WESTBROOK RETIRES AFTER 18 SEASONS.** Westbrook announced his retirement Wednesday via a social-media video narrated by Michael B. Jordan, walking away despite reported interest from the Kings and Wizards this summer. He leaves as the NBA's all-time triple-double leader (209), 5th all-time in assists, and 14th all-time in scoring — a former MVP and 9-time All-Star. *(NBA.com, NBC Sports, Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* One of the most divisive, explosive players of his generation goes out on his own terms instead of chasing one more contract — a genuine end-of-an-era moment.

- **KAWHI LEONARD SPOTTED COURTSIDE IN TORONTO — FUELS RAPTORS TRADE OPTIMISM.** Leonard sat courtside at the National Bank Open in Toronto with partner Kishele Shipley, widely read as a sign he expects his trade to the Raptors to eventually close. The trade (sending Leonard to Toronto for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, and picks) remains paused pending the NBA's cap-circumvention investigation into his Aspiration and Daktronics deals. *(Yahoo Sports, BasketballNews, The Athletic)*
  - *Why it matters:* First real body-language signal from Leonard himself since the investigation froze the trade — Toronto reporters immediately read it as a good sign.

- **GREEK CLUB OWNER CONFIRMS HE TRIED TO BUY JOKIC OUT OF HIS NUGGETS CONTRACT.** Panathinaikos owner Dimitrios Giannakopoulos said he made a "serious" offer to Denver to buy out the final year of Nikola Jokic's deal and bring him to Athens. The Nuggets declined quickly, and Jokic has said he plans to stay in Denver for the rest of his career — but Giannakopoulos says he'll try again once Jokic reaches free agency in 2027. *(ESPN, CBS Sports, ClutchPoints)*
  - *Why it matters:* A wild, out-of-nowhere subplot — a European superclub publicly chasing the reigning-caliber MVP is a first for the league, even if nothing comes of it.
