Beal, 33, had declined his $5.6M player option in the summer after playing only six games in 2025-26 before a season-ending hip injury. - **HALL OF FAME ENSHRINEMENT WEEKEND UNDERWAY — AMAR'E, CANDACE, D'ANTONI HEADLINE.** The Naismith Memorial Basketball Hall of Fame Class of 2026 is being formally enshrined Saturday, August 15, at Symphony Hall in Springfield, MA. Nine inductees: Amar'e Stoudemire, Candace Parker, Chamique Holdsclaw, Elena Delle Donne, Doc Rivers, Mike D'Antoni, Mark Few, Joey Crawford, and the 1996 USA Women's Basketball team. *(NBA.com, WWLP, NBC Sports)*
  - *Why it matters:* Stoudemire and D'Antoni represent one of the most explosive offensive duos in modern NBA history — a genuinely feel-good story and strong content for the channel.

- **STEPH CURRY EXTENSION ELIGIBLE AUG. 29 — WARRIORS CONFIDENT A DEAL GETS DONE.** Warriors GM Mike Dunleavy says he's "pretty confident Steph will finish his career" in Golden State. Curry can sign up to a two-year, $136.7M extension starting August 29. Both sides are open to talking, and mutual interest is described as strong. *(Bleacher Report, Yahoo Sports, Warriors confident per eciks.org)*
  - *Why it matters:* With two weeks until eligibility and the season schedule already out, this is the biggest pending contract story left in the offseason.
