## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES

- **NBA CLEARS STEVE BALLMER — NO EVIDENCE HE FUNNELED MONEY TO KAWHI LEONARD.** After an 11-month investigation into alleged salary-cap circumvention via Clippers sponsors Aspiration, Daktronics and Boingo Wireless, the league found no evidence Ballmer or the team secretly paid Leonard beyond his contract. The NBA pushed back on how ESPN's initial report characterized the findings, but the practical outcome is the same: the path is now clear for the Raptors-Clippers trade to close. *(TMZ, ESPN, Last Word on Basketball, Defector)*
  - *Why it matters:* This is the story that's been hanging over the entire offseason — Mathurin's free agency, the Clippers' cap plan, and the Kawhi-to-Toronto trade have all been frozen on this outcome.

- **CAVALIERS TRADE DENNIS SCHRÖDER TO HORNETS FOR TRE MANN.** Cleveland sends Schröder and cash to Charlotte for Tre Mann, saving roughly $6.8M in cap space (swapping Schröder's $14.8M salary for Mann's $8M). *(Hoops Rumors, Yahoo Sports, Bleacher Report)*
  - *Why it matters:* This is a cap-clearing move — Cleveland is actively opening space to chase a sign-and-trade for Jonathan Kuminga or Peyton Watson while it also finalizes James Harden's new deal.

- **THUNDER TRADE ISAIAH JOE TO PISTONS FOR TWO FUTURE SECOND-ROUND PICKS.** The reigning-champion Thunder move on from the 41% career 3-point shooter after four seasons in OKC. *(Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* A depth/cap move for Oklahoma City as they manage their own crowded books; Detroit keeps stacking shooting around its young core.

- **SAN ANTONIO VOTERS REJECT PROPOSED FUNDING VOTE FOR NEW SPURS ARENA.** The city rejected a proposed public vote on arena funding. *(ESPN)*
  - *Why it matters:* A long-term off-court storyline for the Spurs franchise — no immediate roster impact, but worth flagging for the market/relocation chatter it could fuel.
