## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES

- **AUSAR THOMPSON GETS PAID: 5 YEARS, $155M FROM DETROIT.** The Pistons and Ausar Thompson agreed to a fully guaranteed five-year, $155 million rookie-scale extension Friday, per ESPN's report. Thompson made First Team All-Defense and finished third in Defensive Player of the Year voting last season. *(ESPN, Detroit News, NBC Sports)*
  - *Why it matters:* Ausar and Amen are now the first pair of brothers in NBA history to each land a $100M+ deal — roughly $363M between them this offseason. And Detroit paid the defender before paying the center.

- **KEYONTE GEORGE LOCKED UP: 5 YEARS, $157.5M WITH UTAH.** The Jazz and George agreed to a five-year, $157.5M rookie extension with no player or team options. George averaged career highs across the board last season: 23.6 points, 6.1 assists, 3.7 rebounds on 45.6% from the field and 37.1% from three. *(NBA.com, Hoops Rumors, RealGM)*
  - *Why it matters:* Utah just committed nine figures to a 22-year-old lead guard. The rebuild officially has a franchise centerpiece — and a price tag to defend.

- **JALEN DUREN STILL UNSIGNED — AND THE PISTONS JUST PAID SOMEBODY ELSE.** Duren remains without a deal as his $9.6M qualifying offer deadline approaches. He's reportedly seeking near-max money (around five years, $200M); Detroit is roughly $10M a year short. Reports say he's seriously weighing the QO and hitting unrestricted free agency next summer. *(CBS Sports, Yahoo Sports, NBC Sports)*
  - *Why it matters:* Detroit extending Ausar Thompson on the same day Duren sits unsigned is a loud statement about internal priorities. This is the biggest unresolved contract fight left in the league.

- **NBA CLOSING IN ON A LAS VEGAS EXPANSION OWNER.** Per reporting circulating Friday, the league is focused on finalizing a Las Vegas ownership group first, then turning to Seattle. Vegas bids are approaching or exceeding $10 billion; Seattle is closer to $7 billion. Adam Silver has said he hopes for a decision by end of 2026. *(HoopsHype, NBC Sports, Spokesman-Review)*
  - *Why it matters:* Two new franchises means an expansion draft, 30 protected lists, and a whole new market for stars. The Sonics coming back is one of the biggest stories in the sport.
