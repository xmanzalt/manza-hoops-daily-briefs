## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES

- **THE STEPH CURRY CLOCK STARTED SATURDAY.** Curry officially became extension-eligible on Sept. 19 and can now sign a two-year deal worth up to roughly $136.7M any time before June 30. He is playing out the final year of his current contract at $62.6M; with no extension he hits unrestricted free agency next summer. *(SF Chronicle, NBC Sports Bay Area, SI)*
  - *Why it matters:* Golden State reportedly isn't negotiating in the normal sense — they've handed Curry a **menu**: one-year max, two-year max, or wait. The most decorated player of his generation is being allowed to name his own price and his own timeline. Nothing is signed.

- **JALEN DUREN STANDOFF NOW HAS A HARD DEADLINE: OCTOBER 1.** Detroit's latest offer is in the five-year, $190M range. Duren's camp has held at $200M — $40M a year — since the start of summer. Reporting says the Pistons have **no plans to raise the offer before camp**. *(Yahoo Sports, NBC Sports, PistonPowered)*
  - *Why it matters:* If nothing is signed by Oct. 1, all signs point to Duren taking the $9.6M qualifying offer. That makes him an unrestricted free agent next summer **and** hands him veto power over any trade Detroit tries to make. Nine teams plus a cap-space Sacramento are waiting.

- **BRANDON MILLER IS THE NEXT EXTENSION DOMINO.** Shams reported talks between Miller's camp and Charlotte are expected to intensify over the coming days, with growing optimism a deal gets done. Miller averaged 20.2 points, 4.9 rebounds and 3.3 assists last season on 38.3% from three. *(ESPN/Shams via Hoops Rumors, HoopsHype, RealGM)*
  - *Why it matters:* The No. 2 pick from the 2023 class is the last big name from that draft without a number attached. After Friday's four deals, Charlotte is now the team on the clock.

- **CAMP ROSTERS FILLED OUT LEAGUE-WIDE.** Saturday was an Exhibit 9/Exhibit 10 day: the Pacers added four, the Warriors added one, the Knicks set up a three-man fight for one spot, and Atlanta cut a guard loose. *(Hoops Rumors, HoopsHype)*
  - *Why it matters:* Media day is Sept. 28 and camps open Sept. 29. Every roster spot still open right now is a job interview starting in nine days.
