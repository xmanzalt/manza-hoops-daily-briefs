## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES

- **HAWKS & HORNETS TALKING HIELD ↔ FINNEY-SMITH.** *(RUMOR)* Atlanta and Charlotte have discussed the swap "on and off" all summer, per Jake Fischer via The Stein Line. Atlanta wants to shed Buddy Hield's $9.7M salary. Finney-Smith's $13.3M for 2027-28 is **non-guaranteed**, so the Hawks save money long term. An earlier version of the deal included Devin Carter, whom Atlanta has since waived. *(Hoops Rumors / Stein)*
  - *Why it matters:* It's the only live trade framework right now. It's also more proof that Charlotte (LaMelo Ball and Miles Bridges already gone) is still reshaping its roster around Brandon Miller.

- **DWIGHT POWELL GOES BACK TO DALLAS.** The 35-year-old center, a Maverick for 12 seasons, is expected to sign a non-guaranteed camp deal with **Exhibit 9** injury protection, per Marc Stein. He practiced at Kyrie Irving's player-run mini-camp in LA this week. Dallas is now at 19 players with 14 guaranteed contracts, so without a trade he's a **long shot** to make the opening roster. *(Hoops Rumors / Stein)*
  - *Why it matters:* A loyalty story. The longest-tenured Mav returns on a camp deal just to compete for a roster spot.

- **KAWHI–RAPTORS EXTENSION: STILL "ADVANCED."** Stein's Sunday notes say talks are advanced. The most Toronto can offer is about **two years, $124M**. Stein adds that the Clippers had declined to give Leonard any extension, which set up the trade to Toronto. *(Hoops Rumors / Stein)*
  - *Why it matters:* Toronto paid Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick and two first-round picks for Kawhi's final contract year. An extension would make that trade worth it.
