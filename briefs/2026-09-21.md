## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES

- **KAWHI LEONARD SPEAKS — "MY SON WAS BORN HERE."** Kawhi sat down for the Raptors' in-house "Open Gym" show and explained exactly why he came back to Toronto: *"This city took a liking to my family, and I took a liking to this city… My son was born here, and he's been asking me forever when he's going to come back."* Extension talks between his camp and the Raptors are described as "advanced" per Jake Fischer / The Stein Line. Kawhi earns $50.3M in 2026/27 — his last year — and can extend for up to ~$124M over two years (capped by extend-and-trade and Over-38 rules). *(Hoops Rumors / The Stein Line, The Athletic/Koreen)*
  - *Why it matters:* The Raptors gave up Brandon Ingram and two first-round picks to get him — they need an extension badly, and his quotes sound like someone who intends to stay.

- **STEPH CURRY: CLARITY EXPECTED "IN THE NEXT WEEK AND A HALF."** Appearing on CNBC Thursday to announce a new Li-Ning shoe deal, Curry reaffirmed he's a Warrior for life — *"I've always stated that I wanna be a lifer with Golden State."* ESPN's Anthony Slater then said on NBA Today: *"Steph Curry, at some point, I would guess, in the next week and a half, will come to some clarity about if he wants to do an extension now or wait."* Training camp opens Sept. 29 — so a decision is coming fast. *(Hoops Rumors, CNBC, ESPN/Slater)*
  - *Why it matters:* "Week and a half" from Sunday Sept. 20 = a decision by roughly Sept. 28 — which is media day. The whole league is watching.

- **JIMMY BUTLER UPDATE: "GOOD UPDATES," ORGANIZING PRE-CAMP EVENT.** Warriors star forward Jimmy Butler (ACL tear) continues to receive encouraging reports on his recovery, per Slater, but will be limited to individual work when camp opens Sept. 29. Notably, Butler is organizing a players-only pre-camp event in Southern California next week for the Warriors roster. *(Hoops Rumors / ESPN/Slater)*
  - *Why it matters:* Butler leads even while hurt. And his status is the single biggest roster question for Golden State's season.

- **TACKO FALL IS BACK IN THE NBA.** The seven-foot-six center signed a training camp (Exhibit 10) deal with the Philadelphia 76ers alongside guard Jameer Nelson Jr. and forward Saint Thomas. Fall last played in the NBA in 2021-22 with Cleveland; he's been playing in the CBA with the Ningbo Rockets. He was once teammates with new 76er Jaylen Brown in Boston. *(ESPN, NBC Sports, Yahoo Sports)*
  - *Why it matters:* Great content story — the tallest guy in basketball is coming back, and he now shares a locker room with his former Celtics teammate and one of the faces of the new-look Sixers.
