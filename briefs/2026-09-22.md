## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES

- **PISTONS RAISE DUREN OFFER TO 5 YEARS, $200M FULLY GUARANTEED, AND HE STILL SAYS NO.** Detroit went up $10M from its $190M August offer. The new deal has no player or team options and averages $40M a year. Duren has rejected it, per The Athletic's Sam Amick & Hunter Patterson. Shams: *"It's been all about respect"* for Duren, and just business for the team. It goes back to a ~$20M-a-year "take-it-or-leave-it" extension offer a year ago. Amen Thompson's 5yr/$208M Rockets deal is the comparable in the room. *(Hoops Rumors, Yahoo Sports, B/R)*
  - *Why it matters:* The **Oct. 1** qualifying-offer ($9.6M) deadline is 9 days away. Stein says Duren is "increasingly prepared" to play on the QO and become an **unrestricted** free agent in 2027. Rival teams think he's "ready for a change of scenery."
- **NICOLAS BATUM RETIRES AFTER 18 SEASONS.** He announced it Monday in a social-media video with his son: *"I think I've done my time."* He's 37, played 1,205 regular-season games (Blazers, Hornets, Sixers, two stints with the Clippers) and won Olympic silver with France in 2020 and 2024. *(ESPN, NBA.com, France24)*
  - *Why it matters:* One of the great glue guys of his era, and a France national-team legend. It's a European angle that fits your audience.
- **DOC RIVERS: "I'M ABSOLUTELY RETIRED."** The Bucks fired him after last season. He's joining NBC as a game analyst, alongside his son Austin on the network's roster. His career record is 1,194-866, with a 2008 title and a 2026 Hall of Fame induction. *(ESPN, AP, Awful Announcing)*
  - *Why it matters:* One of the most polarizing coaches of the century is done coaching, and he'll now be the one analyzing coaches on national TV.
- **CLIPPERS NAME JOHN GIBSON INTERIM GOVERNOR & CEO.** Gibson is an LA trial lawyer and former DLA Piper litigation partner who has held Clippers season tickets for years. The NBA approved Ballmer's nominee. He takes over basketball and business operations immediately while Ballmer serves his one-year suspension over the Kawhi cap-circumvention case. *(ESPN, CBS Sports, Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* A lawyer is running an NBA team during an active federal investigation. That tells you where the Clippers' priorities are right now.

After eighteen seasons, more than twelve hundred games, and stops in Portland, Charlotte, Philadelphia and two stints with the Clippers, Batum announced it in a video with his son.
