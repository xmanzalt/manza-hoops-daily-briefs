## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES

- **KAWHI SIGNS ON TO FINISH IN TORONTO: 2 YEARS, $115M.** He agreed to an extension that kicks in for 2027-28; 2028-29 is a player option. Counting his $50.3M this season, that's 3 years/$165M total. He took ~$11M under his max and had already waived a $7.45M trade kicker. - **TRADE: HAWKS SEND BUDDY HIELD + RYAN NEMBHARD (+ cash) TO CHARLOTTE FOR DORIAN FINNEY-SMITH.** It's a 2-for-1 that fixes Atlanta's 16-guaranteed-contract crunch. Charlotte is expected to waive Nembhard, which makes it his second trade in under three months. *(ESPN, NBA.com, Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* The Hornets get shooting after trading LaMelo Ball this summer. Atlanta gets a 3-and-D wing and avoids eating guaranteed money.
- **ROCKETS KICK OFF MEDIA DAY SEASON — "MAKE SOME REAL NOISE."** Ime Udoka, after two straight 52-win seasons and first-round exits: *"We're not here to just make the first round."* Fred VanVleet (ACL) and Steven Adams are healthy again, and Marcus Smart has been added. *(ESPN/AP, NBA.com)*
