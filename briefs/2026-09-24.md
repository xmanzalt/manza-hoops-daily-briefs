## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES

- **KAT–KNICKS EXTENSION TALKS "HAVE STAGNATED."** Shams and Vincent Goodwill report that no deal is likely before camp. The Knicks have discussed around $45–50M a year. Towns makes $57M this season, has a $61M player option for 2027-28, and is eligible for about $275M over four years. *(ESPN via Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* Owner James Dolan wants to stay out of the second apron after the title run. A 31-year-old star on a possible expiring deal adds a real storyline for the champs.
- **CURRY: "NO DRAMA," BUT NO DECISION.** Nearly four weeks after he became extension-eligible, Steph says it will happen "at the right time, once I have all the information." Golden State has laid out several options: one or two years at the max, less than the max, or waiting until June 30, 2027. He says he feels "no hesitation" physically after missing 39 games last year. *(ESPN's Anthony Slater via Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* Depending on what Curry and the Butler and Draymond contracts do, the Warriors could open up **$100M+ in cap space** next summer.
- **DUREN–PISTONS: IS THE RELATIONSHIP BROKEN?** Duren turned down 5yr/$200M. Reports now say the offer had **weight clauses** that frustrated him, and The Athletic's Sam Amick says extending **Ausar Thompson first** "added a layer" to the talks. Commentators (not insiders) are calling the relationship possibly "not repairable." *(The Sporting News via Yahoo)*
  - *Why it matters:* The All-NBA center can sign the qualifying offer (about $9.5M) by Oct. 1 and become an unrestricted free agent next summer. That would be a disaster for a 60-win #1 seed.
- **NBA PROBE INTO THE BUCKS' GARY TRENT JR. DEAL IS STILL OPEN.** A league spokesperson confirmed it. The law firm Hecker Fink is investigating possible cap circumvention in Trent's 4yr/$64M guaranteed deal, which came after a minimum deal in 2024 and $3.7M in 2025. The Bucks and Klutch are cooperating. *(The Athletic's Joe Vardon via Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* The Clippers' Kawhi case cost them five firsts and $30M. Milwaukee could be looking at the same kind of penalty.
