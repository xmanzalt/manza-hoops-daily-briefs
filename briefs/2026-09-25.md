## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES

- **KAT–KNICKS EXTENSION COULD DRAG INTO THE SEASON.** Sources now say the two sides may not get a deal done before camp — and possibly not before the season starts. Towns is eligible for roughly $276M over four years; the Knicks have been offering closer to $45–50M/year. *(Hawaii Tribune-Herald/AP, Posting and Toasting)*
  - *Why it matters:* New York just won its first title since 1973 (Jalen Brunson was Finals MVP over the Spurs). Ownership wants to stay under the second apron even as it tries to keep the core of a champion together.
- **PISTONS PUT A WEIGHT CLAUSE IN JALEN DUREN'S OFFER — AND IT BLINDSIDED HIM.** The Athletic reports Detroit's 5-year, ~$35M/year proposal (Duren wants $40M+) included weight-based incentive language, similar to clauses GM Trajan Langdon once used with Zion Williamson. The Pistons reportedly believe Duren's weight hurt him in their second-round loss to Cleveland. *(The Athletic via Detroit Sports Nation)*
  - *Why it matters:* Duren can just sign his ~$9.6M qualifying offer by Oct. 1 and walk into unrestricted free agency next summer — the Pistons can push that deadline to March 1, but a full-season standoff with the Bucks and Kings floated as trade suitors would be a mess for a 60-win team.
- **WIZARDS AND ANTHONY DAVIS OFFICIALLY TABLE EXTENSION TALKS.** GM Will Dawkins confirmed both sides agreed to hold off on AD's ~$275M/4-year extension until the season is underway. Dawkins said Davis will "remind the basketball world who he is," praised new backup center Deandre Ayton as "a starting-level center and probably the best backup" available, and backed head coach Brian Keefe (43-160 since 2023-24) going forward. *(Multiple, via CBS Sports/Yahoo)*
  - *Why it matters:* Davis has played under 60 games in five of his last six seasons — Ayton is insurance, and a bad start could put real pressure on Keefe's seat.
- **THUNDER GM SAM PRESTI EXPLAINS THE SUMMER'S COST-CUTTING TRADES.** At his preseason presser, Presti addressed shipping Lu Dort and Aaron Wiggins to Atlanta and Isaiah Joe to Detroit for seven combined second-round picks and no players back. He called moving Dort "the greatest sign of respect we could've shown Cason [Wallace]," and said the moves keep OKC $6.9M under the second apron ahead of expected max extensions for Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, Jalen Williams, Chet Holmgren and Wallace. *(Yahoo Sports)*
  - *Why it matters:* The reigning-contender Thunder are actively reshaping the bench around their extension math — a preview of the tough calls "second apron" teams face this cycle.
