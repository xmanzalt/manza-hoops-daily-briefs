What is up, Manza Hoops fam. It is Wednesday, September thirtieth, and this is your daily NBA rundown covering Tuesday, day one of training camp.

The big story is in Detroit. Pistons center Jalen Duren is holding out of camp. Quick injury notes. Kawhi Leonard was not a full participant in Toronto's first practice, as the Raptors manage him with the playoffs in mind. Chet Holmgren added two pounds, which is a big deal for one of the thinnest players in the league.

Around the league, Heat coach Erik Spoelstra called his first practice with Giannis Antetokounmpo, quote, surreal. The Knicks' new coach Mike Brown wants to lean less on Jalen Brunson, and in Orlando, new coach Sean Sweeney is getting good early reviews.

On the transaction wire, there were no new trades or signings found for Tuesday.

Looking ahead, camp continues today, preseason reportedly starts October fourth, and the regular season tips off October twenty-first. Watch Duren.
