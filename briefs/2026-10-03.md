## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES

- **VEGAS EXPANSION MAY SLIP TO 2029-30.** Mike Vorkunov of The Athletic reports the NBA has discussed a Las Vegas debut in 2029-30 instead of 2028-29. Nothing is final. He also says the owner pick does not look imminent, though an unofficial agreement could come before the end of November. *(The Athletic via Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* It cools Shams' "very near future" line from this week. The price is still $12–13B: about $10B expansion fee plus a $2–3B arena.
- **GRIZZLIES WAIVE JORDAN HAWKINS, THREE WEEKS AFTER TRADING FOR HIM.** Shams reports a mutual decision so the 2023 lottery pick can find a new team. Memphis eats his $7.02M salary. *(ESPN; Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* A 24-year-old shooter hits the market in camp. He averaged 5.1 points in 51 games last season, down from 9.2 over his first two years.
- **NBA WILL NOT PUNISH THE WIZARDS OVER TANKING REMARKS.** Bub Carrington told WUSA-TV the team was "trying to get AJ Dybantsa. That's no secret." The Athletic reports no discipline is expected for him or the team. *(The Athletic via Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* A player said the quiet part out loud, and Trae Young agreed on a podcast. The league treats a player's comment as less serious than an owner's.
- **THE GIANNIS ERA STARTS TONIGHT.** Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bam Adebayo are expected to play in Miami's preseason opener against Toronto in Quebec City. *(RotoWire; Axios Miami)*
  - *Why it matters:* First look at Giannis in a Heat uniform, in the first NBA game ever played in Quebec City. Stars are not expected to play much past the first quarter.
