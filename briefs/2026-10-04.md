## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES

- **GIANNIS DEBUTS FOR MIAMI: PERFECT FROM THE FIELD, 7 ASSISTS IN 14 MINUTES.** Heat beat the Raptors 129-105 in Quebec City. Giannis had 9 points (3-of-3 FG, 3-of-4 FT), 7 assists, 4 rebounds in 14:07, first half only. *(Sun-Sentinel via Yahoo; Yahoo Sports; RotoWire)*
  - *Why it matters:* He played as a passer first. Miami scored 41 in the first quarter and 76 by halftime, and at one point had assisted on 17 of its first 19 baskets.
- **KAWHI SITS — AND WILL LIKELY SIT AGAIN ON OCT 10 AGAINST THE CLIPPERS.** Darko Rajaković said before the game Leonard would not play and probably will not play in Vancouver either. RotoWire lists it as not injury-related. *(Canadian Press via CP24; RotoWire)*
  - *Why it matters:* The Vancouver game is against his old team, the one he and the league spent two months of the summer untangling. Toronto is being very careful with a 35-year-old.
- **SPOELSTRA COMPARES GIANNIS' PASSING TO MAGIC JOHNSON.** "With the size and the vision and his ability just to put it on the numbers to your shooters is really impressive." *(Sun-Sentinel via Yahoo)*
  - *Why it matters:* That is the Heat telling you the plan: Giannis as the hub, shooters around him. Wiggins hit four threes, Hardaway three.
- **GIANNIS ON HIS FUTURE: "IF I'M GOING TO BE HERE FOR A YEAR OR TWO OR WHATEVER."** He holds a $62.8M player option for next season and is not committing past it. *(Miami Herald via Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* Miami gave up Herro, Ware, Jaquez, Jakučionis and five picks. The debut was great; the contract clock is already running.

## 🏀 GAME RECAPS & SCORES

**Heat 129, Raptors 105** — Videotron Centre, Quebec City (first NBA game ever played there)

- **Miami:** 41 points in the first quarter, 76-55 at the half. Starters and main reserves sat the whole second half. *(All U Can Heat; Hot Hot Hoops)*
- **Heat starters:** Giannis, Bam Adebayo, Andrew Wiggins, Davion Mitchell, Klay Thompson.
  - Giannis: 9 pts, 7 ast, 4 reb, 3-of-3 FG, 14 minutes.
  - Adebayo: 10 pts, 6 reb, 2-of-7 FG.
  - Wiggins: 12 pts, 4-of-6 from three.
  - Thompson: scoreless, 0-of-2. Mitchell: 4 assists.
- **Heat bench:** Tim Hardaway Jr. 14 pts (4-of-4 FG, 3-of-3 from three, 11 minutes). Pelle Larsson 13 pts (3-of-3 FG, 7-of-7 FT). Nikola Jović 6 reb, 3 ast.
- **The sloppy part:** Spoelstra said Miami had about 25 turnovers. He used all 19 available players.
- **Toronto starters:** Scottie Barnes, RJ Barrett, Immanuel Quickley, Jakob Poeltl, Ja'Kobe Walter.
  - Barrett: 15 pts, 3-of-9 FG, 7-of-8 FT, 17 minutes.
  - Barnes: 11 pts on 5-of-11. Quickley: 5 pts.
  - Chucky Hepburn: 11 pts, 5 reb, 3 stl in 25 minutes off the bench.
- **Side note:** The Heat's flight landed in Quebec City at 12:57 a.m. Saturday after a delay of more than six hours. *(Sun-Sentinel)*
