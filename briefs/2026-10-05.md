- **MAX STRUS GOES DOWN FIVE MINUTES INTO HIS CLIPPERS DEBUT — NON-CONTACT.** Right foot, straight to the locker room, ruled out. Ty Lue: "Never good to see when it's a non-contact play." Tests come once the team is back in Los Angeles. *(Yardbarker; RotoWire)*
  - *Why it matters:* No diagnosis yet, so do not guess at severity. But it was his first game for a new team, and non-contact foot injuries are the ones teams fear.
