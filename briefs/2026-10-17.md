## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES

- **NBA ORDERS SPORTSBOOKS TO PULL "UNDER" PROP PARLAYS.** Per Shams (ESPN), a memo to partner books bans three bet types: parlays that include the under on the same player's stats, "first of" markets such as the opening tip, and unders on players expected to play under 12 minutes. *(CBS Sports; Yahoo Sports)*
  - *Why it matters:* This is the league's biggest integrity move since the Jontay Porter ban and the 2025 federal betting indictments. The NBA says its partners cover more than 99% of the US betting market.
- **PRESEASON WRAPS WITH A 12-GAME FINALE.** Magic blow out the Heat 136-115 behind Banchero's 24. Celtics beat the 76ers 125-120 in OT despite 27 points and 8 rebounds from Embiid. Bucks edge the Pacers 128-127 in OT with 25 from Herro. *(ESPN scoreboard)*
  - *Why it matters:* These were the last tune-ups. Real games start Tuesday.
- **ESPN INTEL: KNICKS–TOWNS, SEATTLE EXPANSION, ANTHONY BLACK.** Windhorst reports Las Vegas bids could top $10B and a winner could be picked within weeks, while Seattle's bids are below what the league wants. Orlando hasn't offered Black the roughly $22M a year Podziemski got. *(ESPN)*
  - *Why it matters:* Expansion money (about $500–600M per team) is shaping every front office. Black is the extension-deadline case to watch.
