## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES
- **THUNDER GIVE CASON WALLACE A 5-YEAR, $139M EXTENSION.** It's about $27.8M a year through 2031-32, done one day before the deadline. *(ESPN/Shams; Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* Wallace made All-Defense Second Team last season. With Dort, Joe and Wiggins gone, he's in line for a much bigger role. It also puts OKC on track for a huge tax bill next summer.
- **BEN SIMMONS TEARS HIS RIGHT ACHILLES IN KINGS PRACTICE.** The Kings confirmed it. He'll have surgery and is expected to miss all of 2026-27. *(Shams; Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* He was set to start on opening night after averaging 10.5 points in two preseason games, his best-looking basketball in years. He's the third player to tear an Achilles this month, after Oweh (OKC) and Hukporti (PHI).
- **STEPH CURRY LIMPS OUT OF WARRIORS PRACTICE: RIGHT ANKLE SPRAIN.** It was a non-contact play at Sunday's open practice. Kerr said Curry told him he's "fine," and a team source called it "not serious." *(ESPN; Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* GSW opens Wednesday at the Lakers. The relief is that it's the ankle, not the right knee that cost him 27 games last season.
- **SUNS EXTEND OSO IGHODARO: 3 YEARS, $29M, fully guaranteed through 2030-31.** Phoenix also picked up his $2.5M option for 2027-28. *(Shams; Hoops Rumors)*

Golden State opens Wednesday against the Lakers, so keep an eye on his status.

And the Lakers are still sitting at sixteen players and still trying to move Dalton Knecht before the four p.m. Central cutdown.
