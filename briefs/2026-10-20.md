## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES
- **HORNETS GIVE BRANDON MILLER A 5-YEAR, $209M EXTENSION.** Shams reported it around 4 p.m. ET, about an hour before the deadline. It's about $41.8M a year and starts in 2027-28. *(ESPN/Shams; Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* Miller is officially the face of Charlotte after the LaMelo Ball trade. It's $1M more than Amen Thompson's deal, making it the second-biggest rookie extension of the year behind Wemby's max. And it comes despite the shoulder surgery in May.
- **TIMBERWOLVES GIVE JADEN McDANIELS A ONE-YEAR, $40M EXTENSION.** It adds 2029-30 and brings him to $124M over four years. HoopsHype's Scotto says it's the first time in NBA history a non-max player with three guaranteed years left has been extended. *(ESPN/Shams; Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* It's a smart contract move. A future extension can start at up to 140% of that $40M salary.
- **CUTDOWN DAY CASUALTIES: Lakers waive Dalton Knecht. Mavs waive Dwight Powell after 12 seasons. Bucks cut Kevin Porter Jr. Grizzlies waive 2025 first-rounder Walter Clayton Jr.** *(Hoops Rumors)*
  - *Why it matters:* LA ate Knecht's $4.2M because no trade came, so Bronny, Jaden Hardy and Vanderbilt stay. Powell was Dallas' longest-tenured player. Clayton was waived after one year.
- **CURRY IS CLEARED:** He was a full participant in practice Monday, and Kerr says he'll play Wednesday at the Lakers. *(Hoops Rumors; B/R)*
