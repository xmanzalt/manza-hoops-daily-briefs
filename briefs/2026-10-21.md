## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES
- **SIXERS SPOIL THE KNICKS' RING NIGHT, 106-100.** New York raised its first title banner since 1973, then lost to LeBron and Jaylen Brown in their Philly debuts. Maxey led all scorers with 28. *(Yahoo; NBC Sports)*
  - *Why it matters:* It was the most-hyped game of the night, and the new-look Sixers looked like a contender on Day 1.
- **PISTONS ERASE A 22-POINT DEFICIT AND BEAT BOSTON, 121-113.** It's the biggest comeback in a season opener of the play-by-play era (since 1997-98). Paul George's **38 points** were the most ever by a Celtic in his debut. *(NBC Sports; ESPN)*
  - *Why it matters:* It's a rematch of two Game 7 losers from last spring. Detroit's defense flipped the game, and Tatum shot 6-for-23 and 0-for-7 from three.
- **THUNDER WIN THE WEST FINALS REMATCH IN SAN ANTONIO, 116-104, AND IT GOT HEATED.** SGA scored 32. De'Aaron Fox threw Chet Holmgren to the floor (Flagrant 1), and Wembanyama plus three Thunder players got technicals. *(ESPN box score; EssentiallySports; ClutchPoints)*
  - *Why it matters:* It's the closing game of opening night and the first chapter of the Spurs-Thunder rivalry this season. Wemby shot 1-for-13 from the field, and OKC got early revenge for last year's 4-3 series loss.
- **ADAM SILVER ON THE McAFEE SHOW:** LeBron's Fanatics helicopter deal is "perfectly appropriate" as long as no team is involved. Expansion is "still a few seasons away," with Las Vegas and Seattle under close review. *(CBS Sports; B/R)*

What's going on, Manza Hoops family. It's Wednesday, October twenty-first, and the NBA is officially back. Opening night gave us three games, a ring ceremony, a record comeback, and LeBron James playing the villain at Madison Square Garden. Let's get into it.

We start in New York. The Knicks got their rings and raised their first championship banner since nineteen seventy-three. Jalen Brunson told the crowd he wants to do it all again. Then the Philadelphia seventy-sixers walked in and spoiled the party, one-oh-six to one hundred. Tyrese Maxey led everybody with twenty-eight points, five assists, three steals and three blocks. In their Sixers debuts, Jaylen Brown had nineteen, eight and six, and LeBron added fourteen points, nine rebounds and seven assists. Joel Embiid chipped in thirteen and eleven. The Garden chanted "we don't want you" at LeBron, and he fired back at a heckler that there's nothing soft in the Midwest. For the Knicks, Brunson scored twenty-one but shot six for nineteen, and Karl-Anthony Towns had nineteen and twelve while stuck in foul trouble. New York got within one twice late, but Edgecombe and Maxey closed it out with dunks.

In the opener, Detroit pulled off something we've never seen on opening day. The Pistons were down twenty-two points to Boston in the third quarter and came all the way back to win, one-twenty-one to one-thirteen. That's the biggest season-opener comeback of the play-by-play era. And it wasted a historic night from Paul George, who scored thirty-eight in his Celtics debut, the most ever by a Celtic in his first game. Jayson Tatum had a rough one, six for twenty-three and oh for seven from three. Cade Cunningham and Jalen Duren each had twenty-two for Detroit, and Javonte Green came off the bench in the second half and dropped nineteen in seventeen minutes.

In the nightcap, we got the Western Conference finals rematch, Thunder at Spurs, and it got heated. In the second quarter, De'Aaron Fox threw Chet Holmgren to the floor, he was called for a flagrant one, and the benches nearly emptied. Victor Wembanyama and three Thunder players, Jaylin, Jalen and Kenrich Williams, all got technical fouls. And Oklahoma City got a little revenge, winning one-sixteen to one-oh-four. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander scored thirty-two, and Jared McCain came off the bench and hit six of eight from three for twenty points. Stephon Castle led the Spurs with twenty-four, and Dylan Harper had eighteen as a starter. Wemby had twelve points, thirteen rebounds and six blocks, but he shot just one for thirteen from the field. Round one goes to the Thunder.

Off the court, Adam Silver went on the Pat McAfee Show. He said LeBron's helicopter deal with Fanatics is perfectly appropriate, as long as no team is involved. He also said expansion is still a few seasons away, with Las Vegas and Seattle at the front of the line.

Quick injury round before tonight. Steph Curry is expected to play, but Brandin Podziemski is out for Golden State's opener with a back issue. Charlotte will be without Kon Knueppel, Grant Williams and Coby White in Brooklyn. Orlando is without Anthony Black and Jonathan Isaac, but Franz Wagner is cleared. And Cleveland's Nae'Qwan Tomlin is out three to four weeks with a back injury.

On the transaction wire, it was option day. New Orleans picked up the options on Jeremiah Fears, Derik Queen and Yves Missi but declined AJ Johnson's, so he hits free agency next summer. Memphis picked up Zach Edey and Cedric Coward, Chicago kept Matas Buzelis and Noa Essengue, and Toronto kept Collin Murray-Boyles and Ja'Kobe Walter. In the rumor mill, Marc Stein says Dallas is the frontrunner to sign Walter Clayton Junior once he clears waivers today. And on Karl-Anthony Towns and that sixty-one-million-dollar option, there's still no answer.

Tonight we get eleven games. The big one is at ten Eastern on ESPN: Steph Curry and the Warriors at the Lakers. We also have Timberwolves at Heat, Mavs at Rockets, and Kings at Clippers to close the night. Then tomorrow, it's Cavs at Sixers and Nuggets at Thunder.
