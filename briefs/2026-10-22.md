The Warriors say they're not expecting him around the team any time soon. The Bucks were contacted and reportedly aren't interested, and Golden State's hard cap makes any deal complicated. Before anything else, we're wishing Kristaps the best.

Luka Doncic had twenty-eight, eight and seven in just three quarters. Chicago turned it over twenty-seven times and made just four of thirty-six threes.
