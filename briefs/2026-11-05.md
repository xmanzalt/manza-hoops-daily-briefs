## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES
- **WEMBY'S CAREER NIGHT: 52 POINTS, 24 REBOUNDS, 8 BLOCKS** in a 112-111 win over Dallas. Kyrie missed a turnaround 15-footer with 3 seconds left. *(AP/ESPN)*
  - *Why it matters:* He's only the second player with 50 points, 20 rebounds and 5 blocks in a game since blocks became official, after Bob McAdoo in 1974. He joins Wilt and Elgin Baylor as the only players with 50 and 20 in a one-point win. He had 29, 15 and 5 blocks in the FIRST HALF alone.
- **WARRIORS STOP PAYING KRISTAPS PORZINGIS.** Golden State is withholding his semimonthly checks on a $20M salary, ruling him "not rendering his services." He left the team before camp and is reportedly leaning toward retirement. *(Shams Charania & Anthony Slater, ESPN)*
  - *Why it matters:* His status changed from "illness" to "not with team." The Warriors aren't voiding the deal; they're shopping the near-expiring contract before the Feb. 11 deadline. The players' union isn't fighting it.
- **SIXERS WIN THE EAST SHOWDOWN, 112-106 IN DETROIT.** Jaylen Brown had a season-high 30 and blocked Cade Cunningham's 3 with 37 seconds left. Embiid had 26 and 15. Philly is 8-1, and its only loss is to Detroit. *(AP/ESPN)*
  - *Why it matters:* Philly extends its lead atop the East and evens the season series with Detroit.
- **LEBRON MAKES HISTORY: FIRST PLAYER EVER TO 16,000 MADE FIELD GOALS.** A left-handed runner in the second quarter in Detroit got him there. He finished with 13 points, 10 rebounds and 8 assists. *(AP/ESPN)*
- **SGA DROPS 42 AS SHORT-HANDED OKC HOLDS OFF CLEVELAND, 124-116.** Shai had 42 points and 8 assists on 11-of-26 shooting, and the Thunder survived a late Cavs rally. *(AP/ESPN)*
