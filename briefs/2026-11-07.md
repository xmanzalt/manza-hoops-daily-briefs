## 🔴 BREAKING / TOP STORIES
- **KRISTAPS PORZINGIS "STEPS AWAY" FROM BASKETBALL.** He posted that his body "didn't respond" during the ramp-up to camp. It is **not** a retirement. GSW hasn't been paying his $20M salary; neither he nor the union is contesting that, and the team won't void the deal. *(ESPN, Anthony Slater)*
