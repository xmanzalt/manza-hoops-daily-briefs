It's **5 wins in a row** for Memphis. **GSW went 0-5 on the road trip** and shot **5-29 from three**. *(ESPN/AP)*
  - *Why it matters:* **MEM (12-4)** was projected as a lottery team after the Morant trade. Now they're tied with HOU at 3rd in the West.
- **KAT BEATS HIS OLD TEAM AGAIN.** **NYK won 102-99 at MIN**: **Towns 22/12**, **Brunson 21/7**. The Knicks blew most of a **19-point lead** and **didn't score for the final 3:26**.   - *Why it matters:* **NYK has won 3 straight (9-6).** MIN drops to 11-5 while **Ant has "no clue" when he'll be back**.
