On the transaction side it was quiet. No trades. The Kings signed veteran guard Gabe Vincent, and the Bulls waived Orlando Robinson. But the rumor mill is heating up. Rob Pelinka says the Lakers will be active before the deadline, and Marc Stein says they want a backup center, though they're short on picks. Stein also says the Clippers are viewed as a certain seller, with Hachimura, Lopez, Strus and Derrick Jones all available, and that the Nets could shop Michael Porter Junior and Julius Randle. On Dejounte Murray, The Athletic says there is zero traction on a Jalen Green swap with Phoenix. And these are rumors: the Nuggets and Magic have shown interest in free agent Kevin Porter Junior.

The drama of the weekend was Draymond Green against Eddie Johnson. They went back and forth online for five hours on Sunday morning, and then Draymond won the game with that final stop and said, if I flex my resume back, I'm the bully. JJ Redick also had a great deadpan about Austin Reaves being out: who would have thought that not having an All-Star level player would hurt your offense.
